# History section: AI image & video prompts

Use these prompts to create AI images and videos for the **History (आपला इतिहास)** timeline. Upload them in **Admin → History → Edit entry → Image / Video**.

> **Please label AI media honestly.** These are *illustrations* of our story, not historical photographs. Start each entry's content with "AI illustration", or use the caption, so nobody mistakes them for real archive images. Never generate images of real, identifiable people (elders, leaders) without their family's permission.

**Tools that work well**
- Images: Google Gemini (Imagen), ChatGPT (image generation), Ideogram, Leonardo, Midjourney.
- Video (5–10 s clips): Google Veo, OpenAI Sora, Runway, Kling, Luma.

**Settings**
- Images: 16:9, at least 1600 px wide. The site compresses any size automatically.
- Videos: 16:9, MP4, up to 300 MB.

**Shared style line.** Add this to every prompt so the set looks consistent:

> *Style: warm cinematic illustration inspired by Warli art of Palghar, earthy terracotta and ochre palette, soft golden light, painterly texture, respectful and dignified, no text, no logos.*

## 1. मेवाडची मुळे · Roots in Mewar, Rajasthan
**Image prompt**
> A majestic hill fort in Mewar, Rajasthan at sunrise, inspired by Chittorgarh, with a tall carved victory tower, golden desert light, a small group of traders with turbans and bullocks setting out from below the fort, dust in the air, wide establishing shot. *+ style line*

**Video prompt**
> Slow aerial push-in over a Rajasthan hill fort at sunrise; below, a line of traders and pack-bullocks begins walking out onto the plain; warm dust, birds crossing the sky. 8 seconds, cinematic, gentle camera move. *+ style line*

## 2. बैलांचे तांडे · The bullock-caravan traders
**Image prompt**
> A long caravan ("tanda") of hundreds of pack-bullocks carrying jute sacks of rice and wheat, crossing the Sahyadri ghats on a winding dirt road. Herders in dhotis and pagadis walk with sticks; green monsoon hills, mist in the valleys. *+ style line*

**Video prompt**
> Side-tracking shot following a bullock caravan along a ghat road; bells on the bullocks swing, herders walk alongside, clouds drift over green hills. 8 seconds. *+ style line*

## 3. मोरडी बंदर · Trading at Moradi port (Murbe)
**Image prompt**
> An 18th-century Konkan coast trading beach at Moradi (today Murbe near Boisar, Maharashtra): wooden sailing boats with lateen sails, bullock carts unloading grain sacks, traders bargaining, coconut palms, golden evening light over the Arabian Sea. *+ style line*

**Video prompt**
> Waves rolling onto a Konkan beach as a wooden sailing boat arrives; bullocks with grain sacks wait on the shore while traders wave. 8 seconds. *+ style line*

## 4. आगगाडी आली · The railway changes everything
**Image prompt**
> A 19th-century steam train crossing the Konkan countryside, white smoke trailing, while a herder and his resting bullocks watch from a field: the end of an era. Late-afternoon light. *+ style line*

**Video prompt**
> A vintage steam locomotive passes from left to right across paddy fields; in the foreground a bullock lifts its head to watch it go. 8 seconds. *+ style line*

## 5. चोवीस गावे · Twenty-four villages
**Image prompt**
> A peaceful Palghar village during the monsoon: tiled-roof houses, a temple, green paddy fields being planted by farmers in a row, bullocks ploughing, mango trees, children on a mud path. *+ style line*

**Video prompt**
> Slow drone glide over green paddy fields and a small Maharashtrian village in light rain; farmers plant rice seedlings in a line. 8 seconds. *+ style line*

## 6. शिक्षण आणि उद्योग · Education and industry
**Image prompt**
> 1970s Boisar: first-generation college graduates in simple clothes holding certificates in front of a village school, a modern industrial skyline and power-station domes in the distance, hopeful mood. *+ style line*

**Video prompt**
> Students throw graduation caps in the air in front of a school building; the camera tilts up to reveal factories and power-station domes on the horizon at golden hour. 8 seconds. *+ style line*

## 7. आजची नवी पिढी · Today's new generation
**Image prompt**
> A joyful community gathering in a Palghar village: families of three generations in traditional Maharashtrian attire, a Tarpa dance circle, some young people with laptops and phones, festive lights. Warm, proud, inclusive. *+ style line*

**Video prompt**
> A circle of villagers dances the Tarpa dance around a musician at dusk; the camera rises to show lights of the village and a modern town beyond. 8 seconds. *+ style line*
